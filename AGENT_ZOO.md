# Agent Zoo desk (Anth.us)

Adapted from Chatticus marketing blog editor guide:
`Chattic.us-web/content/blog/AGENTS.md` (News desk: Updates and Agent Zoo).

Direct, clear, no hedging, no emojis. Write with a genuine **sense of wonder and
excitement** about workplaces where people and bots collaborate — curious and
alive, not hype, not a press release.

## The beat

A product category is forming: persistent multi-agent workplaces — named bots
that hold jobs, share a computer, run in the background, and do useful work
together. One good picture of the same idea: a reactor chamber for AI agents to
collaborate and do useful work. The industry has not settled on a word. The
thing is stable; the jargon moves.

On anth.us, **Agent Zoo** is the name for that category beat: peers, patterns,
and useful work in the wild. Anthus writes as a **participant**, not a detached
reviewer or press office.

## Public name is Agent Zoo

Never name this desk "Model Zoo" or "the model zoo." Model zoo is one name in
the wild for a related idea; it is not ours.

## Synonyms the desk covers

Names you will see in the wild for the same idea. They are not names for the
desk. Keep this as a living list; extend it when a new coinage names the same
workplace where agents collaborate and do useful work:

- software factory
- bot farm
- model zoo (one name in the wild; not ours)
- agent org
- reactor chamber
- foundry
- shop floor
- yard (as in shipyard: concurrent work in one place)
- studio

Add further synonyms when they name the same idea: a workplace where agents
collaborate and do useful work.

Chatticus research note (source): `Chattic.us-web/content/wiki/names-for-the-workplace.md`.

## Who is in the zoo

Coverage includes Anthus products (Chatticus, Tactus, Kanbus, …) and peers such
as Gastown, Grok Bot, and PostHog's agent/cowork work, plus new entries as they
appear. This desk MAY name third-party products. That does NOT license renaming
Anthus product concepts (bots, computers, skills, routines, protocols) after them.

Dated coverage of a vendor belongs in a **dated article or short post**, not as
a permanent "vendor homepage" on anth.us. When the piece is about an *idea*
(farm, factory, shared computer), say so clearly in the framing.

## Product notes vs Agent Zoo

**Product / delivery posts** — Anthus's own progress, ship notes, how we build
and operate. Honesty pattern: live / proven / shipping. Checkable claims.

**Agent Zoo** — the trade desk about the category. Anthus product changelog does
not belong here as the main point. Generic LLM or model-release news does not
belong unless it changes how a farm actually runs.

Many anth.us pieces sit on the border; pick the primary desk by what the reader
is meant to take away (our ship vs the category).

## Voice

- **Wonder and excitement.** The category is new and strange in a good way.
  Carry curiosity and forward energy — what these workplaces make possible —
  without empty superlatives.
- Anthus is a participant, not a press office and not an outside reviewer of
  itself.
- Warm communal register ("people and bots").
- No hedging empty-states ("coming soon", "we're just getting started").
- At most one "X, not Y" contrast per page.
- Claims about Anthus products must be checkable against code, docs, or
  production.

## How to publish on anth.us

1. Author MDX under `anthus-site-content` (`*.mdx` articles or `posts/`).
2. Follow Anth.us ship-preview gate before calling anything live (cover,
   `og:image`, `twitter:card=summary_large_image`, production URL).
3. Newsroom tracking: Kanbus key `ANTH` in
   `AnthusAI/anthus-semantic-knowledge-base`.
4. Unpublished work stays off Anth.us Gatsby `main` until Ryan says ship;
   content `main` deploys the live site.
