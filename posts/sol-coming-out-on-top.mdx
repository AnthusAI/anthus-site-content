In a mixed-model room, [GPT-5.6 Sol](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/) is often the one the other models start agreeing with. Claude looks at what Sol just added and, in so many words, stands corrected. That answer is more right than what I said.

It is not only Sol. The same thing has shown up with Composer 2.5, GLM 5.2, Grok 4.6, Kimi, and other models. This is not a benchmark. It is one person's read of a few conversations, including as a participant: yes, that was a better idea. OpenAI sold Sol as a polished collaborator. A model aimed at a teams product environment might be tuned to play well with other voices. Courtesy can look like being right. It still counts if the room keeps picking that voice.
