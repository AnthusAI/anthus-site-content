Perplexity has [announced](https://x.com/AravSrinivas/status/1835874778768625683) a new "Reasoning" focus feature, powered by OpenAI's o1 preview model. It's a way to put o1's reasoning capabilities directly into Perplexity's search interface.

The new Reasoning focus, utilizing the o1 model, brings sophisticated problem-solving skills to Perplexity. Users can expect more precise and reliable answers to complex queries, benefiting from the model's advanced reasoning capabilities and improved accuracy. Perplexity has built the feature directly into their existing interface, so there's no separate mode to learn. Currently, Pro customers are allocated 10 uses of the Reasoning focus with o1 per day.

This update highlights Perplexity's commitment to providing specialized tools for specific tasks. The o1 model's advanced reasoning capabilities make the new Reasoning focus particularly well-suited for complex problem-solving, and Perplexity's platform gives users an accessible way to put those capabilities to use.

As AI technology continues to evolve, integrations like this show how a strong model and a user-friendly platform can combine into something practical. Perplexity's introduction of the Reasoning focus, powered by the OpenAI o1 preview model, is a good example of advanced AI made accessible to a broader audience.
