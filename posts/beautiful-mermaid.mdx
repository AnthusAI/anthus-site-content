[Beautiful Mermaid](https://agents.craft.do/mermaid) is an open source Mermaid renderer from the Craft team. Sixteen themes. SVG and ASCII. Flowcharts, sequence, class, ER, state, and charts. The [source is on GitHub](https://github.com/lukilabs/beautiful-mermaid).

The useful part for agents is the output: a file, not a screenshot of a website.
