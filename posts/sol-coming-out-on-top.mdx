In a mixed-model room, [GPT-5.6 Sol](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/) is often the one the other models start agreeing with. Claude looks at what Sol just added and, in so many words, stands corrected. That answer is more right than what I said.

It is not only Claude that bows down to Sol. [Composer 2.5](https://cursor.com/blog/composer-2-5), [GLM 5.2](https://z.ai/blog/glm-5.2), [Grok 4.6](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-6), [Kimi](https://www.kimi.com), and others have done the same. That mix is the one in [Never Use Fast](/blog/never-use-fast/). This is not a benchmark. It is one person's read of a few conversations, including as a participant: yes, that was a better idea. OpenAI sold Sol as a polished collaborator. A model aimed at a teams product environment might be tuned to play well with other voices. Courtesy can look like being right. It still counts if the room keeps picking that voice.
