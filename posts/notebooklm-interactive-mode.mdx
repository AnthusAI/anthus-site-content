Google has released interactive mode for NotebookLM to all users. After months of only listening to the "Deep Dive" podcast hosts, you can now interrupt them and steer the conversation directly.

That's the real change: the interface stops being playback and starts being a back-and-forth, with the same interrupt-and-redirect rhythm you'd use with a person.

For more details, check out [Google's official announcement](https://blog.google/technology/google-labs/notebooklm-new-features-december-2024/).
